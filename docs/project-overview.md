## Company

Excess Energy is a Nigerian clean energy company. It sells and professionally installs reliable, affordable clean energy systems for homes, businesses, and institutions.

- Name: Excess Energy
- Tagline: "Power Beyond Limits."

This is a real company. The site is treated as a premium commercial product, not a demo or portfolio piece. The reference bar is set by Tesla Energy, Schneider Electric, EcoFlow, Huawei Digital Power, and Enphase.

## Mission and Vision

- Mission: deliver reliable, affordable, professionally installed clean energy for homes, businesses, and institutions.
- Vision: become one of Africa's leading clean energy companies.

## Market and Coverage

- Current focus: Ogun State, Lagos, and Ibadan.
- Future focus: nationwide Nigeria.

Coverage is presented so that new regions are additive. The structure that supports future location pages (for example "Solar Installation in Lagos") is reserved now and implemented later. See the SEO section in [Architecture](./architecture.md).

## Website Goal

The site exists for conversion, not information. The journey is:

Learn, then Trust, then Request a Free Energy Assessment, then Contact, then become a customer.

Every design and engineering decision is judged against whether it moves a visitor along that path. See [UX and Conversion](./ux-and-conversion.md).

## Audience

Homeowners, business owners, schools, churches, hospitals, factories, and property developers.

Device reality: many visitors use mid-range Android phones. The site is designed mobile first and must stay fast on low-end hardware. See performance goals in [Coding Standards](./coding-standards.md).
